01 — UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

OUR PLANET
IS CHANGING.

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperature, weather patterns and natural systems. Human activities have accelerated these changes.

🌡️

RISING TEMPERATURES

Earth's average temperature is increasing, affecting ecosystems, communities and natural systems.

🌊

RISING SEA LEVELS

Melting ice and warming oceans contribute to rising sea levels around the world.

🔥

EXTREME WEATHER

Climate change can increase risks from heatwaves, floods, droughts and intense storms.

🌳

LOSS OF NATURE

Changes in climate can disrupt habitats, ecosystems and biodiversity.

02 — THE CAUSES

WE ARE PART
OF THE PROBLEM.

Modern society depends heavily on activities that release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

01

🏭 INDUSTRY

Industrial activities consume energy and produce greenhouse gas emissions. For example, air pollution from factories causes gas release such as carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide.

02

🚗 TRANSPORT

Cars, trucks, ships and aircraft contribute to emissions through fossil fuel use. For example, cars and motorcycles burn lots of petrol and releases carbon dioxide(CO2) to the atmosphera.

03

🌳 DEFORESTATION

Destroying forests reduces the planet's ability to naturally absorb carbon dioxide. For example, trees absorb carbon dioxide(CO2) from the atmosphera.When forest are destroyed,less (CO2) were removed.

04

⚡ ENERGY

Burning coal, oil and natural gas releases large amounts of greenhouse gases.For example, increases demand for electricity in homes, factories, and businesses leads to more fossil fuels being burned.

05

🌾 AGRICULTURE

Agriculture and livestock can contribute methane and other greenhouse gas emissions. For example, livestock farming.Animal such as cows and sheep produce methane(CH4) during digestion. Methane is a powerful greenhouse gas.

06

🗑️ WASTE

Poor waste management contributes to pollution and greenhouse gas emissions.For example, poor waste management. large amounts of unmanaged waste can increase pollution and greenhouse gass emissions
